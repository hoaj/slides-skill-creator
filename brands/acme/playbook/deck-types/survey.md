## Interview
### replace: arc
Always use: Finding, Meaning, Recommendation.

### after: evidence
id: sample-size
Ask for the number of responses and the data source.

### guide: decision-owner
Name the person who acts on the recommendations.

## Outline
1. Cover
2. Key finding
3. Method and data
4. Findings with their strength
5. Recommendations

## Content rules
- Show n and the data source on every chart.
- Say how strong each finding is.
- Never quote a person in a way that can identify them.
