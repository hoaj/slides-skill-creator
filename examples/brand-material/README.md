# Sample brand material

Material for two fictional brands, in the shape a maintainer brings it to the Generator. Use it to
try the Generator's modes and to test them: `new` for `acme`, `add-brand` for `tidewell`, and
`edit-brand` to give `tidewell` a font.

| Brand | Material |
|---|---|
| `acme/` | a PowerPoint template with the theme colours, theme fonts and a PNG logo on the master; written guidelines; the SVG logo; Public Sans as `.ttf`; twelve icons |
| `tidewell/` | written guidelines with hex colours and system fonts, and the SVG logo |

The finished `acme` Brand pack is in [`brands/acme`](../../brands/acme). Licences: Public Sans is
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`acme/fonts/OFL.txt`) and the icons are Phosphor Icons under
MIT (`acme/icons/LICENSE.txt`). Everything else here is original to this project.
