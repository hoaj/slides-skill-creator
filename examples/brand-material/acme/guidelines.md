# Acme brand guidelines

Acme is a fictional company. Everyone calls it Acme; in contracts it is Acme Corp, and older
material says Acme Inc.

## Colour

Our palette is quiet with two loud notes.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #14213D | Text, and our dark backgrounds |
| Paper | #FFFFFF | The page |
| Mist | #EEF1F7 | A soft background for a break from white |
| Cobalt | #2447D8 | Our brand blue: headings, links, a bold cover |
| Sun | #FFC233 | Highlights. Use sparingly: once per document |
| Dusk | #2A1458 | The base of our Dawn gradient |
| Coral | #FF7A59 | Only inside the Dawn gradient |
| Teal | #0F766E | Charts only |

Dawn is a gradient of coral and cobalt glows over Dusk. Use it for covers and big statements, and
keep long text away from its bright corner.

Body text is always Ink on light backgrounds and Paper on dark ones. Cobalt and Sun are for
headings, numbers and graphics, never for paragraphs.

## Type

Public Sans, regular and bold, for everything. The font files are in `fonts/`.

## Logo

The logo is a rising triangle and a drawn wordmark. It is one colour and takes the text colour of
its background. Use `logo.svg`; the PNG in the PowerPoint template is for Office only.

## Presentations

- One idea per slide. Prefer a chart or a number to a paragraph.
- Decks are marked INTERNAL unless they are cleared as PUBLIC. Some decks carry no label.
- We present in English.
- Our decks usually go to the leadership team, a product team or customers, and they always ask
  for a decision. Say who makes it.
- We tell a story in three steps: Context, Choice, Consequence.
- We run a customer survey every quarter, and its report always has the same shape: the key
  finding first, then method and data, the findings with how strong each is, and our
  recommendations. Every chart shows the number of responses and the source, and we never quote
  a customer in a way that identifies them.

## Icons

We use the Phosphor icon set, regular weight. A selection is in `icons/`.
