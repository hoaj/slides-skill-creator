# Tidewell visual identity

Tidewell is a fictional water utility. People also say Tidewell Water.

## Colours

- **Deep** #0B3B4F: our main colour. Text on light backgrounds, and dark slides.
- **Foam** #F4F8F7: the light background.
- **White** #FFFFFF
- **Reed** #2E7D5B: secondary, for accents on light backgrounds.
- **Signal** #F2A33A: warnings and the one number that matters on a slide.

A slide is light (Foam or White, Deep text) or dark (Deep, white text). On dark slides the accent
is Signal.

## Type

We use the system's sans-serif fonts for everything. We have no font files.

## Logo

`logo.svg`, one colour, taking the text colour.

## Presentations

Decks are marked as OPEN or RESTRICTED; the default is RESTRICTED. Numbers use a space between
thousands and a comma for decimals: 12 500,5.
